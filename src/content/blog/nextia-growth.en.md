# My Experience at Nextia: A 5-Year Journey

At Nextia, I had the opportunity to grow exponentially. I started as a passionate developer and, thanks to my dedication and consistent results, quickly rose to become **Head of Engineering & Operations**. During this time, I didn't just build software; I built the teams and processes that allowed the company to scale sustainably.

## Key Projects of National Impact

I led high-importance national projects, facing complex technical and operational challenges:

- **Infonavit**: I coordinated 5 different projects, modernizing interfaces and optimizing critical workflows for the institution. We implemented microservices architectures that significantly improved system scalability.
- **Senate of the Republic**: We implemented complex infrastructure and software systems. I worked directly with directors to ensure security and availability met the most demanding government standards.
- **Currie & Brown**: Technical management and deployment of financial and project analysis tools, integrating various data sources to facilitate managerial decision-making.

## The Art of Leading Teams

My greatest achievement was consolidating and directing a multidisciplinary team of over **40 developers**, including Frontend, Backend, Architects, and DevOps profiles. 

My focus has always been:
1. **Inspire and Motivate**: I firmly believe that a motivated developer can solve any problem.
2. **Technical Orchestration**: Ensuring that every piece of the technical symphony fits perfectly to meet client expectations.
3. **Culture of Collaboration**: I implemented a culture where knowledge is shared and errors are seen as learning opportunities, which drastically reduced team turnover.

## Conclusion

These years at Nextia forged my profile as a leader capable of making high-level administrative and technological decisions, always maintaining a focus on quality and value delivered to the end user.
