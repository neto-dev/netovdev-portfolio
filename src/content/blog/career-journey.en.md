# Ernesto Valenzuela: A Profile Forged in Practice and Passion

Many know me as Ernesto, but truthfully, I prefer to be called **Neto**. If I had to define myself, I wouldn't do it by the titles I've held, but by the goals I've achieved and the challenges I've overcome. My history with technology didn't start in a corporate office; it began much earlier, driven by an insatiable curiosity and an almost impulsive need to turn ideas into reality.

## The Origin: The Spark and the Necessity

My path as a technical leader began almost by accident at age **15** at Colegio La Salle in Durango. It was there that my teacher, Abel, planted the seed of programming. But the true trial by fire came at age **17**, when my first son was born. At that moment, programming stopped being just an interest and became the engine of my life.

Those early years were characterized by voracious learning. While my peers followed traditional paths, I devoured courses, books, and tutorials. I learned backend, frontend, and infrastructure the hard way—solving real problems on the fly. This stage taught me something fundamental: **skills can be learned, but personality and drive are inherent.**

## The Rise: From Developer to Orchestrator

Over the years, I moved through companies like **Plif.mx**, **4 + 1 Creative**, and **Nextia**. In each of them, I started in the trenches of development and, thanks to a mix of boundless energy and a natural ability to inspire, I quickly scaled to leadership roles.

I eventually led multidisciplinary teams of over **40 people**, including architects, DevOps, and senior developers. My primary activity shifted from writing lines of code to **orchestrating the technical symphony**. I have managed projects for highly renowned institutions such as the **Mexican Senate**, **Infonavit**, and **Afirme**.

## Setbacks and Lessons: The Humility of a Leader

It hasn't all been a linear path. I've had setbacks—moments where technical complexity or managing expectations challenged my resolve. But it's precisely in those moments that I've grown the most. I learned that being the technical reference doesn't mean having all the answers, but knowing how to find them and, more importantly, how to guide others so they can grow too.

My work philosophy is based on **empathy and a culture of mutual help**. In all my teams, I've sought to consolidate an environment where we all teach and we all learn. I believe that a leader who doesn't inspire is simply a task manager.

## Who is Neto Today and what does he bring to your company?

Today, I am a balance between a high-level professional and a family man. My time is split between managing teams, talking with strategic clients, and enjoying time with my children. This personal stability is what gives me the clarity to make high-impact technological and administrative decisions every day.

**What Ernesto can achieve for your company:**
- **Impulsive yet Analytical Execution**: I have an almost physical need to put ideas into practice and see results.
- **Inspirational Leadership**: I don't just manage personnel; I build high-performance cultures based on trust and personal growth.
- **Total Versatility**: From solving a critical infrastructure problem to negotiating terms with directors and stakeholders.
- **Accelerated Learning**: My trajectory proves that I can master any technology or role with unusual speed.

My career is a testament that, with passion and perseverance, there is no technical limit that cannot be overcome. I am ready for the next big challenge. **Shall we do something interesting together?**
