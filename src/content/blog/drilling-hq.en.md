# Leadership and Innovation at 4 + 1 Creative

As Technical Lead at 4 + 1 Creative, my main focus was to create from start to finish an integrated system responsible for digitizing the processes of a company dedicated to drilling holes for mining. This was a unique challenge that combined management software and specialized hardware.

## Solutions that Transformed Operations

During my tenure, I led the development of multiple critical tools:

- **Mobile Apps with Offline Mode**: We developed a mobile application for field use, where connectivity is nearly non-existent. We implemented a robust synchronization system that allowed operators to record data and automatically sync it when network was detected.
- **Specialized Hardware and Synchronization**: We worked with hardware to measure drilling deviation. My team and I developed the software that synchronized with these devices via Bluetooth and WiFi, allowing real-time visualizations of drilling precision.
- **Inventory Management and Logbook System**: We created a web admin panel for total inventory control of parts, consumables, and the service log for the company's vehicles.

## Leadership in the Field

I directed a team of **6 developers** with a multidisciplinary focus. In this environment, agility was key. I fostered direct communication between hardware and software engineers to eliminate bottlenecks and ensure that the final product was exactly what the client needed for their daily operations.

## Lessons Learned

This project taught me the importance of software robustness in extreme conditions and how technical leadership goes beyond writing code; it's about understanding the business from the roots up to propose solutions that truly impact operational efficiency.
